💊 All Vitamins

Vitamins, their uses, food sources and foods to limit.

Vitamin A

Use: Supports eyesight, skin and immunity.

Food Sources: Carrot, spinach, sweet potato, pumpkin, milk and eggs.

Avoid / Limit: Avoid high-dose Vitamin A supplements unless advised by a doctor.

Vitamin B1

Use: Helps energy production and normal nerve function.

Food Sources: Whole grains, beans, nuts, seeds and pork.

Avoid / Limit: Avoid unnecessary high-dose supplements.

Vitamin B2

Use: Supports energy production and healthy skin.

Food Sources: Milk, curd, eggs, almonds and green vegetables.

Avoid / Limit: Avoid unnecessary high-dose supplements.

Vitamin B3

Use: Supports energy metabolism and skin health.

Food Sources: Chicken, fish, peanuts, whole grains and mushrooms.

Avoid / Limit: Avoid high-dose niacin supplements unless advised by a doctor.

Vitamin B5

Use: Helps the body produce energy and hormones.

Food Sources: Chicken, eggs, mushrooms, avocado and whole grains.

Avoid / Limit: Avoid unnecessary high-dose supplements.

Vitamin B6

Use: Supports brain function, metabolism and red blood cell formation.

Food Sources: Banana, chicken, fish, potatoes, nuts and chickpeas.

Avoid / Limit: Avoid long-term high-dose B6 supplements.

Vitamin B7

Use: Supports metabolism, skin and hair health.

Food Sources: Eggs, nuts, seeds, sweet potato and legumes.

Avoid / Limit: Avoid unnecessary high-dose biotin supplements.

Vitamin B9

Use: Important for cell growth and blood formation.

Food Sources: Leafy vegetables, beans, peas, oranges and fortified grains.

Avoid / Limit: Avoid high-dose folic acid supplements without medical advice.

Vitamin B12

Use: Supports nerves and red blood cell formation.

Food Sources: Milk, curd, eggs, fish, chicken and other animal foods.

Avoid / Limit: Avoid taking high-dose supplements without professional advice.

Vitamin C

Use: Supports immunity, skin health and iron absorption.

Food Sources: Orange, lemon, amla, guava, tomato and capsicum.

Avoid / Limit: Avoid excessive Vitamin C supplements.

Vitamin D

Use: Supports bones, muscles and calcium absorption.

Food Sources: Sunlight, fortified milk, eggs and fatty fish.

Avoid / Limit: Avoid high-dose Vitamin D supplements without medical advice.

Vitamin E

Use: Supports skin health and antioxidant protection.

Food Sources: Almonds, sunflower seeds, peanuts, spinach and avocado.

Avoid / Limit: Avoid high-dose Vitamin E supplements unless advised.

Vitamin K

Use: Supports normal blood clotting and bone health.

Food Sources: Leafy green vegetables, broccoli, cabbage and spinach.

Avoid / Limit: If taking blood-thinning medicine, keep Vitamin K intake consistent and follow medical advice.
